# Colgrid Pricing

Sep 28, 2026 · Matt [last name]

Every gathering is $75 per player, or $280 for all four with a Season Pass.

**Pilot (October 2026):** one Eventbrite ticket, Colgrid Pilot at $75/person. The Season Pass goes on sale after the pilot has run and been tested. Eventbrite's U.S. fees (3.7% + $1.79 per ticket, plus 2.9% payment processing) are passed to the buyer, so they don't come out of the margins below.

 There are no other discounts or early-bird prices. Every ticket plays casually by default; entering the tournament is optional and costs nothing extra.

| Product | Price | Cost to serve | Margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gathering ticket | $75 per player | $40 | $35 (47%) |
| Season Pass (4 gatherings) | $280 ($70 per gathering) | $160 | $120 (43%) |
| Flagship | $725 per player | ~$420–$545 (estimate) | $180–$305 (25–42%) |
| Corporate private run | $2,500 for up to 20, +$95 per extra player | $960 + $16 per player | $1,220 at 20 (49%) |
| BID / tourism sponsorship | $5,000–$10,000 per season | Impact report prep | Most of it |

Ticket-platform fees are paid by the buyer at checkout, so they don't come out of these margins.

## What a ticket includes

- One full gathering, 2–4 hours, played with your team
- 3–4 hosted quests and all quest materials
- Finale meal and first drink at the closing ritual
- Numbered session badge and printed quest passport
- Your web pass: team, XP, level, badges, and progress toward the flagship

Season Pass holders also get a guaranteed team spot at all four gatherings, a Founding Badge on their web pass, and priority toward the flagship.

## After the pilot: Vanguard membership ($360 / year)

Launches once a second city opens: 4 gatherings in any city, 1 guest seat per year, a founding field kit (budget ≤ $25), flagship priority plus an extra Explorer lottery entry. Cost to serve $225; margin $135 (38%), or $175 (49%) if the guest seat goes unused.

## Unit economics

At $75 and 40 players, a session brings in $3,000, costs $1,600, and keeps $1,400 (47%). Break-even is 17 players.

| Cost item | Per player | Per session (40 players) | Fixed or per head |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Host payments | $18 | $720 | Fixed per session |
| Game master and pre-game emails | $6 | $240 | Fixed per session |
| Finale meal and drinks | $12 | $480 | Per head |
| Keepsakes and badges | $4 | $160 | Per head |
| **Total** | **$40** | **$1,600** | $960 fixed + $16 per head |

## Flagship

$725 per player, whether they earned a Championship spot through the tournament or were drawn in the Explorer lottery. Estimated cost per person for 2 nights: lodging $150–$275, meals ~$120, hosts and venues ~$100, keepsakes and staff ~$50 (total ~$420–$545). Confirm with real venue quotes.
